About Us

Our Mission

St. Monica Catholic Youth Parish is a vibrant community located in Ongata Rongai, Kware, dedicated to nurturing the faith of young people. We provide a welcoming space where youth can encounter Christ, grow in their Catholic identity, and become leaders in their communities.

Inspired by St. Monica, whose persistent prayer and faith led to the conversion of her son St. Augustine, we believe in the power of faith, community, and perseverance.
